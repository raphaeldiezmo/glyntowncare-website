Visiting Hours

Visiting Times

Glyntown Care warmly invites visitors during designated hours: 11:30 am to 12:45 pm and 2:30 pm to 4:30 pm. Beyond that, a flexible open visiting period begins at 6:00 pm, allowing families and friends the opportunity to spend quality time with residents.

Bus Route Option

  • Look for the nearest bus stop to Glyntown Glanmire that is serviced by the bus 214. You can use a map or a transportation app to locate the nearest stop.
  • Board the bus 214 at the bus stop and make sure it is headed to hazelwood not Knockraha. Always ask the driver.
  • Stay on the bus until you reach the last stop.
  • Open Google Maps or visit glyntowncarecentre.ie and scroll down to the map at the end of the home page for directions.
