Careers at Glyntown

Working with Us

At our nursing home, we believe that our team members are the heart and soul of our organization. As a leading care provider in our community, we are committed to delivering the highest quality of care to our residents while fostering a positive and supportive work environment for our staff. Joining the health care team means becoming part of a dedicated family that shares a common goal — providing compassionate and exceptional care to those who need it most.

Staff Perks

Free Meals

We take pride in providing complimentary, delicious meals to our dedicated staff as a token of appreciation for their hard work and commitment.

Free Uniforms

Well-designed uniforms take into consideration the comfort of employees during their work hours. Healthcare uniforms are designed for ease of movement and comfort.

Pension Plan

We prioritize our staff's long-term financial well-being by offering comprehensive pension plans, empowering them to secure a stable and fulfilling future.

As a festive gesture, we gift complimentary Christmas vouchers to our staff in appreciation of their hard work and dedication.

Our work culture is rooted in a set of core values that guide us in everything we do

  • Compassion

    We treat every resident and colleague with the same kindness and respect that we would expect for our own loved ones.

  • Excellence

    We are committed to delivering the best possible care by continuously striving for excellence in all aspects of our work.

  • Teamwork

    Collaboration is at the heart of our success. We recognize that every role within our organization is essential, and we work together seamlessly.

  • Professionalism

    We maintain the highest standards of professionalism and integrity, ensuring that our actions align with our commitment to quality care.

  • Growth

    We provide opportunities for personal and professional growth, supporting our team members in their pursuit of continuous learning and development.

Why Choose a Career with Us

  • Meaningful Work: Every day, you'll make a difference in the lives of our residents and their families. Your contributions will directly impact their well-being and quality of life.
  • Supportive Environment: We foster a collaborative and inclusive work environment where open communication, mutual respect, and teamwork are valued.
  • Training and Development: We are dedicated to helping you grow in your career. We offer ongoing training and development opportunities to enhance your skills and knowledge.
  • Career Paths: Whether you're just starting out or looking to advance your career, Glyntown Care Centre offers various career paths within caregiving, nursing, administration, and more.
