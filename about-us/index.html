Glyntown Care Centre

Exceptional Care

Compassionate, person-centred nursing home care in the heart of Glanmire, Co. Cork

Discover more

At Glyntown Care Centre, we are proud to provide a warm, welcoming environment where residents receive high-quality, person-centred care tailored to their individual needs. Whether you're looking for long-term residential care, short-term respite, or specialised dementia support, our dedicated team is here to help every step of the way.

Our purpose-built facility offers comfortable accommodations, beautiful landscaped gardens, and a range of engaging activities designed to promote well-being and social connection. From daily living assistance to 24/7 nursing support, we focus on dignity, independence, and enhancing quality of life.

At Glyntown, we don't just care for people — we care about them. We are a home, a community, and a place where everyone is treated like family.

A Place of Trust and Transparency

We value open communication, working collaboratively with families to understand residents' needs, and ensuring a dedicated team is always available to enhance the quality of life for those in our care.

Our Mission

Our mission is to offer compassionate, personalized care in a supportive environment. We enhance residents' well-being through activities and services that promote physical, emotional, and social health. Our dedicated team respects each resident's dignity and fosters strong relationships with families and the community, ensuring a safe, comfortable home.

Our Commitment to Holistic Well-being

We believe in holistic well-being, addressing physical, emotional, social, and spiritual needs. Our approach involves tailored healthcare, diverse activities, and programs for a purposeful and joyful living experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the leading provider of exceptional senior care, offering a warm, supportive, and empowering environment. We are committed to excellence, innovation, and compassionate service, ensuring our residents thrive while fostering trust with families and the community.

What We Offer

Services and Facilities

We provide compassionate care that goes beyond the ordinary, fostering a haven of wellness and support for your loved ones' journey to a brighter and healthier future.

24/7 Nursing Care & Medical Cover

Our elderly residents receive round-the-clock nursing care, comprehensive medical coverage, and access to our dedicated GP as well as our multidisciplinary services - Speech and language therapy, dietician, occupational therapy, podiatrist optician, dentistry and community x-ray.

Structured Care for Daily Living

We provide personalised daily routines for elderly residents in our nursing home, ensuring they receive the care and attention they need to maintain their physical and emotional well-being.

Engaging Activities for Resident Well-Being

We provide a wide array of enjoyable activities such as baking, dancing, singing, crafting, arts, gardening, outings, garden parties, watching films, and listening to music, promoting well-being and happiness.

Empowering Self-Care

We provide support for our residents' self-care by offering hairdressing, hand massage, physiotherapy, and chiropody services on a quarterly basis, recognizing the importance of self-care in enhancing well-being during the golden years.

Convenient Laundry Care

Our nursing home provides convenient and reliable laundry services to our residents, ensuring their comfort and cleanliness with the exception of dry cleaning.

Spiritual Nourishment

Our nursing home provides regular mass services every month, complete with visiting priests and the opportunity for Holy Communion and receiving the Sacrament of the sick, allowing our residents to engage in meaningful spiritual practices.
