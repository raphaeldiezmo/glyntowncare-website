Our Services

Services and Facilities

We provide compassionate care that goes beyond the ordinary, fostering a haven of wellness and support for your loved ones' journey to a brighter and healthier future.

24/7 Nursing Care & Medical Cover

Our elderly residents receive round-the-clock nursing care, comprehensive medical coverage, and access to our dedicated GP as well as our multidisciplinary services — Speech and language therapy, dietician, occupational therapy, podiatrist optician, dentistry and community x-ray.

Structured Care for Daily Living

We provide personalised daily routines for elderly residents, ensuring they receive the care and attention they need to maintain their physical and emotional well-being.

Engaging Activities for Resident Well-Being

We provide a wide array of enjoyable activities such as baking, dancing, singing, crafting, arts, gardening, outings, garden parties, watching films, and listening to music.

Empowering Self-Care

We provide support for residents' self-care by offering hairdressing, hand massage, physiotherapy, and chiropody services on a quarterly basis.

Convenient Laundry Care

Our nursing home provides convenient and reliable laundry services to our residents, ensuring their comfort and cleanliness with the exception of dry cleaning.

Spiritual Nourishment

We provide regular mass services every month with visiting priests, Holy Communion, and receiving the Sacrament of the sick.

Our Facilities

Facilities

Our facilities are designed to meet the needs and enhance the quality of life for our residents.

24 Hour Nursing Care & Doctor on call
Professional medical care available around the clock for residents' health and well-being.
Nurses' Station and Office
A central hub where staff coordinate care and attend to residents' needs.
Nurse call system in each bedroom and bathroom
Emergency call buttons ensure quick assistance when needed.
Secure courtyard with fountain and outdoor seating
A peaceful outdoor space for relaxation, fresh air, and socializing.
Private Visitor's Area
A quiet and comfortable space for family and friends to visit residents.
Locking Door Release System
Enhances security, ensuring a safe environment for all residents.
Wheel-chair accessible communal toilets
Designed for ease of use and accessibility for all mobility levels.
Sitting room with plasma TV
A cozy common area for entertainment, relaxation, and social interaction.
